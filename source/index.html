02 · The bigger lever

Efficiency does not come from chatting.
It comes from skills & agents.

Three easy steps to better AI utilization. Every employee can do all three, with the no-code on-board tools we already have or could have.

STEP 1

Use it right

Good prompts, the right model for the task, documents as context. Minutes saved every day. By everyone.

STEP 2

Build your own agents & skills

Turn a recurring task into a reusable skill. No code, directly in Possehl GPT or the chatbot at your availability. Build once, benefit daily.

STEP 3

Tap into existing agents

Possehl GPT and other chatbots at your availability already offer agents built by colleagues or built for tasks like yours. You review the result instead of doing the work.

01

What is hardest every day?

What keeps eating time and annoys you? That is your first candidate.

02

What could automation look like?

Which tools are available?

that actually works.
