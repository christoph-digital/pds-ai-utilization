07 · Your roadmap

Everyone needs a roadmap.

Four questions are enough to start — today, for everyone:

01

What is hardest every day?

What keeps eating time — and annoys you? That is your first candidate.

02

What could automation look like?

What would the result be if the task did itself? A rough sketch of the target picture is enough.

03

Which tools are available?

Possehl GPT, Claude, ChatGPT, Dokko, n8n — the right tool is usually already on board.

04

Ask the AI how to do it.

The simplest trick: Describe the task to the AI and ask it how to automate itself.

No big project needed. Four questions — and your first skill is live.
